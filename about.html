← LIBRARY HOST LOCAL

A small local internet.

Inspired by Amsterdam’s neighbourhood book cabinets, HOST lets people leave something for whoever comes next.

Wake

Press the device’s button to open a temporary local Wi-Fi network. Connect and open the library in your browser.

Share

Every book holds a file and its conversation. Leave one, download one, or add a comment. Downloads stay in the library.

Make room

The library holds 500 MB. When it fills, books with the fewest downloads and comments leave to make space for new ones.

BUILD YOUR OWN

Make a HOST.

Based on an ESP32-S3, a TF card, a button, and a ring of light. The project is open to build and adapt.
